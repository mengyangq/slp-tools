Tools for speech-language pathology

A growing collection of free, browser-based tools for speech-language pathology teaching and clinical training.

A growing set of small, focused web tools for SLP students and clinicians. Each one opens in your browser, works on your own transcripts and data, and needs no account or install.

Private by design. Every tool runs entirely on your device. Child and clinical language samples are never uploaded to any server.

ChatLens

Language sample analysis for CHAT/CLAN transcripts. Upload a .cha file and see MLU, morphemes, lexical diversity and more, with a word-by-word transcript view showing exactly how every number is derived, and worked formulas that plug in the file's own numbers.

  • MLU · MLT · MLU5
  • Brown’s morphemes
  • Lexical classes
  • TTR · MATTR · VOCD
  • Inline word tagging
  • Worked formulas
Open ChatLens → Works offline after first load · sample transcripts included
